My Story & Mission

Every Saturday afternoon, my family hops on a WhatsApp call to check in with relatives back home in Egypt. It is our primary bridge across the world—a time for sharing stories, catching up, and watching my five-year-old cousin play with video filters while yelling "Eftahu el camera!" Yet, far too often, the video freezes and the audio cuts out due to underdeveloped regional infrastructure. Experiencing this gap firsthand sparked my core passion: leveraging engineering to ensure that distance never compromises human connection. At its heart, wireless communication isn't just about signal strength or data rates; it's about keeping families connected, regardless of geography or economic conditions.

My journey toward solving these global infrastructure disparities began at NYU through the Wireless 6G Vertically Integrated Project (VIP), where I research and model high-frequency calibration kits using 3D electromagnetic simulators (HFSS). I am pairing this rigorous technical foundation with practical industry experience—focusing on real-time data transmission and applying Machine Learning models to network optimization. Through international exposure, including studying regional infrastructure challenges in the UAE, I am working to understand both the high-level technology and the on-the-ground needs of communities that lack reliable, affordable networks.

Ultimately, my mission aligns with UN Sustainable Development Goal 9: building resilient infrastructure, promoting sustainable industrialization, and fostering innovation. By bridging the digital divide, I aim to design next-generation communication systems that ensure underserved regions are no longer left behind. My goal is to advance global telecommunications so that no child—whether in Egypt, the UAE, Sub-Saharan Africa, or Oceania—has to struggle to see the faces or hear the voices of the people they love.

What is GLASS?

"The Global Leaders and Scholars in STEM (GLASS) program brings together and supports high-achieving undergraduates with the potential for becoming global leaders in innovation, technology, research, and entrepreneurship. Through a variety of programming and resources, the GLASS program empowers students to become the next generation of socially conscious engineering leaders."

Global Competency
